# todo-webapp-2

## Problem Statement

People who want to keep track of small tasks need a quick place to jot them down and tick them off, without the weight of accounts or full task-management tools.

## Solution

A simple single-page web app where one user adds todos, sees them in a list, marks each done or undone, edits its title, and deletes it. Todos persist between visits.

## Actors

- User: anyone who opens the app; sees and manages the todo list. There is no sign-in and no distinction between users.
